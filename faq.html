Prime Global Help Centre

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers about opportunities, applications, member accounts, referrals and withdrawals.

Is Prime Global a job employer?

Prime Global helps members discover and navigate remote-work opportunities. When an opportunity belongs to a third-party employer or platform, that organization controls eligibility, assessment, hiring and payment.

Does creating an account guarantee a job?

No. Creating an account or applying through an external platform does not guarantee acceptance, employment, work volume or income.

How do I find an opportunity?

Use the Opportunities, Start Working and Jobs sections on the main website. Read the requirements carefully and use the official application route provided for the opportunity.

How does the referral programme work?

The current programme proposes a $2 reward for a qualified referral. A referral must meet the stated eligibility conditions, including account verification and completion of a qualifying task that Prime Global reviews and approves.

Is a referral reward automatic?

No. Referral rewards are subject to review and approval. A registration or job application alone does not create an approved reward.

What is the current withdrawal threshold?

The current programme setting is a minimum withdrawal threshold of $30. Available withdrawal methods and eligibility are subject to the applicable Prime Global programme terms.

Can I submit evidence of completed work?

Yes, where Prime Global requests a task verification. Submit only the information needed for review. Never send passwords, one-time codes, identity documents or other sensitive credentials as task evidence.

What happens if an opportunity is no longer available?

External opportunities can close or change without notice. Always confirm the current status on the employer's official page before applying.

Can companies work with Prime Global?

Yes. Companies and project owners can contact Prime Global to discuss potential workforce or digital-work projects. Any future project will be presented according to its actual requirements and agreed terms.

Safety reminder: Prime Global will not ask you to provide passwords or one-time authentication codes. Be cautious of anyone claiming they can guarantee employment or earnings in exchange for money.
