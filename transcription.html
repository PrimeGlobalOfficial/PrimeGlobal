Transcription

Learn what transcription work involves, the skills and equipment commonly required, how assessments work and what to check before applying.

What is Transcription?

Transcription is the process of converting spoken audio or video into written text.

Transcription projects can include interviews, meetings, podcasts, research recordings, conversations, business recordings, educational content and other audio or video material.

Common Transcription Tasks

  • Listening to recorded audio.
  • Typing spoken words accurately.
  • Identifying different speakers.
  • Adding timestamps when required.
  • Following formatting guidelines.
  • Correcting obvious transcription errors.
  • Handling different accents and speaking speeds.
  • Reviewing and proofreading completed transcripts.

Who Can Apply?

Typical requirements may include:

  • Strong listening skills.
  • Good spelling and grammar.
  • Accurate and reasonably fast typing.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Ability to understand different accents.
  • Ability to follow detailed formatting instructions.
  • Reliable internet access.
  • Ability to meet deadlines.
Some platforms accept beginners, while specialized transcription work may require experience, industry knowledge or a qualification test.

Hardware & Software

  • Computer or laptop for most professional transcription work.
  • Reliable internet connection.
  • Good-quality headphones.
  • Modern web browser.
  • Word-processing software.
  • Transcription software or platform-specific tools where required.
Phone-only applicants: Some transcription platforms may offer mobile-compatible work, but serious transcription is generally much easier with a computer, keyboard and headphones. Check the specific platform requirements.

Useful Skills

  • Fast and accurate typing.
  • English grammar and spelling.
  • Listening comprehension.
  • Speaker identification.
  • Proofreading.
  • Research skills.
  • Time management.
  • Attention to detail.

Payments

Transcription platforms can use different payment models, including payment per audio minute, completed task, project or another agreed rate.

Possible payment methods may include:

  • PayPal
  • Payoneer
  • Airtm
  • Bank transfer
  • Other payment providers specified by the platform

Check the current rates, payment schedule, minimum payout and withdrawal requirements before beginning work.

Country & Language Availability

Transcription platforms may restrict registration or projects by country and language.

Some projects may specifically require English, Swahili or another language, while others may require a particular accent or regional knowledge.

For Kenya: Check whether the platform accepts Kenyan workers and whether the specific transcription project supports your language.

How to Avoid Transcription Scams

  • Do not pay a recruiter for guaranteed work.
  • Use the official platform website.
  • Check the domain before creating an account.
  • Never share your account password.
  • Be cautious of unrealistic earnings promises.
  • Read the platform's terms and payment rules.
