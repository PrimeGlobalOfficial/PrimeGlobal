About Prime Global

Connecting people with digital work information and global opportunities.

Prime Global is an online platform focused on helping people discover information about remote work, digital opportunities and online career resources.

Our goal is to make opportunity information easier to discover and understand for people around the world.

Prime Global does not guarantee employment, selection, income or acceptance into any opportunity. Visitors should always review the requirements and verify important details with the organisation offering the opportunity.

Requirements & Hardware

Requirements vary depending on the opportunity.

Internet Connection

A reliable internet connection may be required for remote tasks, assessments and communication.

Computer or Smartphone

Some opportunities can be completed on a smartphone, while others require a computer.

Headphones & Microphone

Voice recording, transcription and communication projects may require suitable audio equipment.

Relevant Skills

Requirements can include typing, language, research, communication, technical or analytical skills.

Identity Verification

Some legitimate platforms may require identity verification before allowing work.

Assessment

Certain opportunities may require tests, qualification checks or sample tasks.

Payments

Understand how payment arrangements may work.

Payment methods and rates depend on the organisation, project, country and type of work.

Possible payment methods offered by third-party platforms may include bank transfers, electronic payment services and other supported payment methods.

Important: Prime Global does not guarantee payment amounts, payment dates or acceptance into any third-party opportunity. Always confirm payment information directly with the organisation offering the work.
