About Prime Global

Helping people discover digital work information and career resources.

Prime Global is an online platform focused on helping people discover information about remote work, digital opportunities and online career resources.

We aim to make opportunity information easier to find and understand for people in Kenya, the Philippines and worldwide.

Prime Global does not guarantee employment, selection, income or acceptance into any opportunity. Always verify opportunities with the organisation offering the work.

Requirements & Hardware

Requirements vary by employer and project.

Internet

A stable internet connection may be necessary for remote work.

Device

Some tasks support smartphones; others require a computer.

Audio Equipment

Voice projects may require headphones and a suitable microphone.

Relevant Skills

Typing, language, research, communication or technical skills may be required.

Identity Verification

Some legitimate platforms verify identity before onboarding.

Assessments

Some projects require qualification tests or sample tasks.

Payments

Understand the payment terms before accepting work.

Rates, payment methods and payment schedules depend on the organisation, project and country.

Depending on the provider, payment options may include bank transfers or supported electronic payment services.

Important: Prime Global does not guarantee payment amounts, payment dates or acceptance into third-party opportunities. Confirm payment terms with the provider. Never pay an unverified recruiter to secure a job.
