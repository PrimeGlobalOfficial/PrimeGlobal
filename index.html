Preparation guide

Common requirements and equipment

Requirements differ by employer, task type, country, and contract.

📋

What you may need

  • A reliable email address and internet connection.
  • A professional CV and accurate application details.
  • Language, typing, research, or task-specific skills.
  • Identity verification where required by the platform.
  • Completion of screening tests or project training.
  • A supported payment method for your country.
🖥️

Hardware you may need

  • Smartphone for mobile-compatible tasks.
  • Laptop or desktop for projects that require one.
  • Headphones and a suitable microphone for audio tasks.
  • A quiet space for recording and calls.
  • Updated browser and secure device settings.
  • Stable internet and power where possible.

Check the specific project requirements before buying equipment.

How payments work

Prime Global currently provides opportunity information and guidance. This website does not currently collect job application fees, accept deposits, process worker payouts, or transfer money.

  • Check the original platform's payment schedule and minimum withdrawal.
  • Confirm which payment providers support your country.
  • Review currency conversion charges and withdrawal fees.
  • Never share banking passwords, PINs, or one-time security codes.
No job, acceptance, task volume, or income is guaranteed. Do not send money based on a promise of guaranteed employment.
