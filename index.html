Typical Requirements & Equipment

Requirements vary by role. Always follow the specific provider's instructions.

Skills & Application Requirements

  • A valid email address and reliable internet connection.
  • Good attention to detail and ability to follow instructions.
  • Language skills appropriate to the role.
  • Accurate profile information and any required identity checks.
  • Completion of any required qualification test or assessment.

Hardware & Software

  • A smartphone may be enough for some recording or simple tasks.
  • A laptop or desktop is required for many annotation and evaluation projects.
  • Headphones or a suitable microphone for audio tasks.
  • A quiet environment for voice recording.
  • Updated browser and any software specifically required by the provider.

International Payment Methods

International digital work providers may support different payout services. The methods available depend on the specific provider, country, currency and account eligibility.

Payment options you may encounter

PayPal Payoneer Airtm Bank Transfer Other Electronic Methods

These are examples of payment services used by some online work providers. They are not a guarantee that every service is available for every Prime Global opportunity.

Before accepting work, confirm the provider's payout method, minimum withdrawal, processing time, country restrictions and fees. Check the payment service's own terms as well.

Payment transparency: Prime Global should only claim to pay participants directly when it actually administers those payments. Where a third-party provider pays workers, that relationship should be explained clearly.
