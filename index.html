General Requirements

Requirements vary by project and company. Always check the official listing before applying.

  • Reliable internet connection.
  • A smartphone may be enough for some projects; others require a computer.
  • Clear communication and attention to detail.
  • Ability to follow project instructions and pass any required assessment.
  • Some projects require specific language skills, location eligibility or identity verification.
  • A payment method supported by the specific platform.
Safety: Never pay an unknown person to "activate" a job. Verify the official website, application page and payment terms before providing sensitive information.

Payments

Payment methods and rates are determined by each opportunity provider.

Prime Global is an information and opportunity directory. We do not control third-party rates, approval decisions or payment schedules.

  • Check the official platform's payment rules before starting work.
  • Confirm minimum payout thresholds and supported countries.
  • Confirm whether your preferred payment method is supported.
  • Keep records of completed work and payments.
