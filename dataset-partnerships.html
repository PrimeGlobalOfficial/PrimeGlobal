For AI companies, research teams and data buyers

We welcome enquiries about existing dataset requirements, licensing discussions and custom collection pilots. To help assess feasibility, please include:

  • Target language, dialect or accent and intended use.
  • Approximate number of speakers, recordings, sentences or words.
  • Required format, audio specifications, transcript format and quality checks.
  • Contributor eligibility, location and any diversity requirements.
  • Consent, usage rights, exclusivity and licensing expectations.
  • Delivery timeline, budget range and procurement process, if available.

Responsible data collection

Voice recordings and language contributions must be collected with informed, documented and voluntary consent. Contributors must understand the intended AI or research use, who may access the data, whether commercial licensing is involved, and any retention or withdrawal terms that apply.

We will not claim ownership of a contributor's voice, collect recordings secretly, or offer identifiable personal information for sale. Any future collection or licensing project must define permissions and safeguards before data is gathered or shared.
