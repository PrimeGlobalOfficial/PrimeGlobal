Post opportunities responsibly

Request employer verification, then submit job listings for review. A submission is not published automatically.

2. Employer verification request

Submit your organization details. Verification status is managed by Prime Global; you cannot approve your own account.

3. Submit a job for review

Important requirements

  • Only submit genuine opportunities you are authorized to advertise.
  • Do not ask applicants to pay to apply or secure a job.
  • Never submit passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive data through this form.
  • Listings are stored as pending review and must be approved through a trusted administrative process before appearing publicly.

Employer verification and job submission are connected to the database. Admin approval tools are not yet included on this page; do not promise a publication date until review is complete.
