MargVoice

MargVoice invites contributors to complete voice-data tasks that help AI systems understand speech, language and natural communication.

Tasks advertised: reading prompts aloud, human–AI conversations and group conversations. Some task types may show as unavailable or coming soon, so check the live task dashboard.

How contributors earn: complete eligible recording tasks and receive rewards for accepted contributions. MargVoice does not publish a guaranteed task volume, fixed rate or guaranteed income on its public homepage.

Application: create an account through the official platform, complete the requested profile or checks, and review available tasks and terms before recording.

Important payment notice for applicants in Kenya: MargVoice payouts are reported by third-party sources to use Wise. Wise's official Kenya website currently states that Kenya does not have Wise account details. This means receiving a platform payout through Wise may not be available to everyone in Kenya, even though some Wise transfer services are available. Before applying or recording, confirm directly with MargVoice that it can pay you using a method you can access. Do not complete substantial work until you have confirmed how you will receive your earnings. Check Wise's official Kenya availability information.

Other payment terms: third-party reviews commonly mention a USD 50 withdrawal threshold, but MargVoice should confirm the current minimum and payout rules directly.

Wise account availability by country — check before applying

Wise features differ by country. The following locations have specific restrictions on holding money or receiving account details:

  • Kenya: Wise says account details are not yet available. Some transfer services are available, but this is not the same as having receiving account details.
  • Bahrain, Israel and Malaysia: holding money is limited to personal accounts; business accounts cannot hold balances.
  • Hong Kong: holding money is available only for business accounts, not personal accounts.
  • India: holding money is available only in specified circumstances, such as while travelling overseas.
  • Thailand: Wise has temporarily stopped issuing currencies and account details.

Wise also does not support its services in certain locations, including Afghanistan, Belarus, Burundi, Central African Republic, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Cuba, Eritrea, Iran, Iraq, Libya, Myanmar, North Korea, Somalia, South Sudan, Russia, Sudan, Syria, Ukraine regions under restriction, Venezuela and Yemen.

These restrictions concern Wise's own features and may change. They do not confirm that MargVoice accepts or rejects applicants from those countries. Check your country's current Wise features and confirm MargVoice's payout method before completing work.
