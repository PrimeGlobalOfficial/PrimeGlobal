Voice Recording

Learn about remote voice-recording opportunities, common recording tasks, equipment requirements, application processes and payment considerations.

What is Voice Recording Work?

Voice-recording projects involve recording spoken audio according to instructions supplied by a company or research project.

Recordings may be used for speech-recognition systems, artificial intelligence training, language research, conversational systems, audiobooks, advertising or other audio applications.

Projects may require individual sentences, scripted conversations, spontaneous speech, narration or other specified recordings.

Common Voice Tasks

  • Reading prepared sentences.
  • Recording short phrases or commands.
  • Recording natural conversations.
  • Recording two-person dialogue.
  • Narrating prepared text.
  • Recording pronunciation samples.
  • Recording local-language or bilingual speech.
  • Providing speech samples for AI training.

Who Can Apply?

Requirements vary by project but may include:

  • Fluent or native-level knowledge of the required language.
  • Clear and understandable speech.
  • Ability to follow recording instructions.
  • Quiet recording environment.
  • Suitable microphone or smartphone.
  • Reliable internet connection for uploading recordings.
  • Ability to complete recordings within the project deadline.
Some projects specifically recruit speakers of particular languages, accents, dialects, countries or demographic groups.

Recording Environment

Audio quality is important. A suitable recording environment should be as quiet as possible and free from unnecessary background noise.

  • Choose a quiet room.
  • Close windows and doors when possible.
  • Turn off unnecessary fans, televisions and other noise sources.
  • Avoid echo-heavy rooms.
  • Keep the microphone at a consistent distance.
  • Follow the project's exact recording instructions.

Equipment & Software

  • Smartphone with a good microphone for projects that permit phones.
  • Computer and external microphone for projects requiring professional recording.
  • Headphones when required for monitoring.
  • Audio-recording software or the project's recording application.
  • Stable internet for uploading files.
Phone-only applicants: Some voice-recording projects are specifically designed for smartphones. Others require a computer or dedicated microphone. Always check the project's equipment requirements.

Audio File Requirements

File specifications depend on the project. Instructions may specify:

  • WAV or another audio format.
  • Specific sample rate.
  • Specific bit depth.
  • Mono or stereo recording.
  • Maximum or minimum recording duration.
  • File naming conventions.

Never change the required format without checking the project's instructions.

Payments

Voice-recording projects may pay per recording, per task, per hour, per project or according to another compensation model.

Possible payment methods may include:

  • PayPal
  • Payoneer
  • Airtm
  • Bank transfer
  • Other payment providers specified by the project

Confirm the payment rate, currency, payment schedule and withdrawal requirements before beginning work.

Country & Language Availability

Voice projects are often highly specific about language, accent, dialect and country.

A project may recruit only speakers from selected countries or regions. Availability can also change as recording targets are completed.

For Kenya: Look for projects requesting English, Swahili or other languages you can genuinely speak at the required level.

Safety Checks

  • Apply through the official company or project website.
  • Do not pay a recruiter for guaranteed acceptance.
  • Do not provide account passwords or security codes.
  • Check the website domain before uploading personal information.
  • Read the project's consent and data-use terms carefully.
  • Understand how your recordings will be used before agreeing.
