Other Remote Jobs

Discover additional categories of online and remote work beyond the main opportunity areas listed on Prime Global.

What Can Be Included?

Remote work covers many different types of jobs. The opportunities below may vary significantly in requirements, experience level, equipment and payment structure.

Virtual Assistance

Administrative and online support for businesses or individuals.

Research Tasks

Online research, information gathering and data verification.

Content Moderation

Reviewing user-generated content according to platform guidelines.

Online Customer Service

Helping customers through digital communication channels.

AI Evaluation

Reviewing or evaluating AI-generated responses and data.

Online Research

Finding, comparing and organizing information from online sources.

Content Writing

Creating articles, descriptions, business content or other written material.

Other Digital Tasks

Short-term or project-based online tasks offered by legitimate platforms.

Who Can Apply?

  • Beginners looking for legitimate entry-level remote work.
  • Experienced professionals seeking remote positions.
  • Freelancers looking for additional projects.
  • People with strong communication or research skills.
  • People with data, administrative, language or technical skills.

Typical Requirements

  • Reliable internet connection.
  • Ability to follow written instructions.
  • Good communication skills.
  • Basic digital literacy.
  • Some jobs require a computer while others may work on mobile devices.
  • Some professional positions require previous experience or qualifications.

Phone or Computer?

Device requirements depend heavily on the job.

Simple research, messaging, surveys, some AI-data tasks and selected microtasks may be possible on a smartphone. More advanced work such as virtual assistance, writing, programming, spreadsheets and professional customer support commonly requires a computer.

How Applications Usually Work

  1. Find a suitable remote opportunity.
  2. Check the country, skill and equipment requirements.
  3. Create an account or submit an application through the official platform.
  4. Complete any required assessments or verification.
  5. Some employers may conduct an interview.
  6. If accepted, complete onboarding and follow the project instructions.
  7. Receive payment according to the employer or platform's terms.

Payment

Remote jobs can use different payment arrangements, including hourly wages, fixed project payments, task-based payments or monthly salaries.

Depending on the provider and country, payment methods may include bank transfer, PayPal, Payoneer, Airtm or other supported services.

Always confirm the current payment method and withdrawal requirements before starting work.

Country Availability

Remote opportunities are not automatically available in every country. Employers and platforms may restrict applications based on location, language, legal requirements, working hours or project demand.

Applicants in Kenya should always confirm that Kenya is supported before completing an application.

Finding Legitimate Work

Use official company websites and established remote-work platforms. Carefully review job descriptions and verify who is offering the work.

Legitimate employers generally should not require unexplained upfront payments simply to apply for a job.

Safety Check

  • Do not pay someone who promises guaranteed employment.
  • Verify the company and application website.
  • Never share passwords, PINs or verification codes.
  • Be careful with requests for unnecessary personal information.
  • Research unfamiliar platforms before providing documents or payment details.
  • Be skeptical of unrealistic income promises.
