Our mission

Our mission is to make global workforce opportunities and useful information easier to discover, understand and navigate. We aim to develop trusted ways for organizations to reach global talent and coordinate human insight responsibly.

We are developing this vision in stages, with an emphasis on clear information, secure member services, transparency and long-term international collaboration.
