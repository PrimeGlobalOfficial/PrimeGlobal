AI Training & Evaluation

Learn what AI training and evaluation work involves, what applicants may need, how assessments usually work, and what to check before applying.

What is AI Training & Evaluation?

AI training and evaluation work involves helping improve artificial intelligence systems by reviewing, rating, comparing, correcting, or creating data used to train and evaluate AI models.

Depending on the project, tasks may include evaluating AI-generated answers, checking whether information is relevant, rating search results, reviewing text, comparing responses, following detailed guidelines, or providing language and cultural feedback.

Who Can Apply?

Requirements vary by company and project, but applicants may commonly need:

  • Good written and spoken English.
  • Strong reading and comprehension skills.
  • Ability to follow detailed instructions.
  • Good attention to detail.
  • Reliable internet access.
  • A suitable computer for projects that require desktop work.
  • Relevant academic, professional, technical, or language knowledge for specialized projects.
  • Residence in a country supported by the particular project.

Some projects accept beginners, while others require previous professional or subject-matter experience.

Hardware & Software

Possible requirements

  • Computer or laptop for many evaluation projects.
  • Stable internet connection.
  • Modern web browser.
  • Email account.
  • Headphones for audio-related projects.
  • Specific software or browser extensions when required by the project.
Phone-only applicants: Some AI-related tasks can be performed on a smartphone, but many professional AI evaluation and annotation projects require a computer. Always check the equipment requirements of the specific project before applying.

Payments

Payment methods and rates depend on the company, project, country, task type, and worker agreement.

Possible payment methods may include:

  • PayPal
  • Bank transfer
  • Payoneer
  • Airtm
  • Other payment providers specified by the platform

Always confirm the current payment method, minimum withdrawal requirements, currency, and eligibility directly with the official platform before starting work.

Country Availability

AI training projects are often restricted by country, language, client requirements, or project availability.

Being able to create an account does not necessarily mean that a project is currently available in your country. Check the specific project requirements before applying.

For applicants in Kenya: Look specifically for projects that list Kenya or worldwide eligibility. Availability can change as projects open and close.

Important Safety Checks

  • Use the company's official website or application portal.
  • Never pay someone simply to obtain a job or guaranteed project.
  • Do not give anyone your password.
  • Be careful with requests for unusual payments or cryptocurrency.
  • Verify the domain before submitting personal information.
  • Read the project terms and payment conditions carefully.
