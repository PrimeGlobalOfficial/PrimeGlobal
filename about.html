About Prime Global

Prime Global is an online platform designed to help people discover remote-work opportunities, learn about digital careers, and find resources for applying to online jobs.

What we cover

  • AI training and evaluation
  • Data annotation and data-related tasks
  • Voice recording and transcription
  • Translation and proofreading
  • Online customer and chat support
  • Other remote-work opportunities and career resources

Our purpose

Our aim is to make information about online work easier to discover and understand. We encourage visitors to read each opportunity's requirements carefully and confirm important details directly with the hiring organisation.

Important information

Prime Global is an opportunity-information platform, not a guarantee of employment. We do not guarantee that a visitor will be hired, earn money, or qualify for a particular opportunity. Unless explicitly stated, we are not the employer offering a listed job.
