Data Annotation

Learn what data annotation work involves, the types of tasks you may encounter, common requirements, assessments, equipment needs and payment considerations.

What is Data Annotation?

Data annotation is the process of labeling or organizing information so that it can be used to train and evaluate artificial intelligence and machine-learning systems.

Depending on the project, workers may annotate text, images, audio, video, documents, search results or other forms of data.

Common Data Annotation Tasks

  • Text classification and labeling.
  • Image classification and object identification.
  • Audio transcription or speech labeling.
  • Video frame annotation.
  • Content relevance evaluation.
  • Sentiment or intent classification.
  • Entity identification.
  • Document and search-result categorization.

The exact task depends on the project and the client's annotation guidelines.

Who Can Apply?

Typical requirements can include:

  • Good attention to detail.
  • Ability to follow written instructions accurately.
  • Good English or another required language.
  • Reliable internet connection.
  • Basic computer skills.
  • Ability to work consistently and meet project quality standards.
  • Residence in a country supported by the project.
Some projects are beginner-friendly, while specialized annotation projects may require technical, linguistic or professional knowledge.

Hardware & Software

  • Computer or laptop for many annotation platforms.
  • Stable internet connection.
  • Modern web browser.
  • Email address.
  • Headphones for audio projects.
  • Project-specific annotation software where applicable.
Phone-only applicants: Some simple annotation tasks may work on a smartphone, but many professional annotation platforms require a computer. Check the individual project requirements before applying.

An available registration form does not necessarily mean that projects are currently available in your country.
