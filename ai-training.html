AI Training & Evaluation

Explore opportunities involving the evaluation, improvement and training of artificial intelligence systems.

What Is AI Training & Evaluation?

AI training and evaluation work involves helping improve artificial intelligence systems by reviewing, rating, comparing or creating data that can be used to make AI systems more accurate and useful.

Depending on the project, workers may evaluate AI-generated answers, review text or images, compare responses, check search results, classify information or provide human feedback.

What You May Need

Language Skills

Strong reading and writing ability in the required language.

Attention to Detail

Ability to follow instructions carefully and identify differences or errors.

Internet Connection

A reliable internet connection is normally required for online projects.

Device

Many projects require a computer, although some opportunities may support mobile devices.
