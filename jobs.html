Types of online work to explore

AI Training and Evaluation

Tasks may include rating AI responses, checking factual accuracy, evaluating search results, and writing feedback to improve AI systems. Some roles require assessments or specialist knowledge.

Data Annotation

Label text, images, audio, or other data according to project guidelines. Work may require attention to detail, training, and passing qualification tests.

Transcription and Translation

Convert audio into written text or translate content between languages. Requirements depend on language pair, accuracy standards, and the client.

Data Entry and Online Research

Organize information, verify records, research public information, or update spreadsheets and databases. Check task instructions and expected rates carefully.

Customer Support and Virtual Assistance

Support customers, manage inboxes, schedule appointments, maintain records, or assist teams remotely. Some positions require set hours and a reliable internet connection.

Freelance Digital Services

Offer writing, design, editing, web support, or other skills to clients online. Build a clear profile and portfolio that accurately reflects your experience.

Finding opportunities from Kenya and worldwide

Before applying, check whether a platform accepts applicants in your country, what equipment and language skills are required, how tasks are assigned, and which payment methods are supported. A platform may be global but still restrict specific projects by location.

How to evaluate an online job

  • Read the eligibility requirements and job description before applying.
  • Confirm payment terms, minimum withdrawal amounts, fees, and payment availability in your country.
  • Never pay an upfront fee to secure a job or release supposed earnings.
  • Use the official platform website and protect your identity documents and account credentials.
  • Do not assume work or income is guaranteed; selection, task availability, and earnings can vary.
Important: Prime Global does not promise that every listed work category has an active vacancy. Always verify the current opening, employer, eligibility, and payment conditions directly with the hiring platform.
