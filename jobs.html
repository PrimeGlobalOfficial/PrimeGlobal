Types of online work to explore

AI Training and Evaluation

Tasks may include rating AI responses, checking factual accuracy, evaluating search results, and writing feedback to improve AI systems. Some roles require assessments or specialist knowledge.

Data Annotation

Label text, images, audio, or other data according to project guidelines. Work may require attention to detail, training, and qualification tests.

Transcription and Translation

Convert audio into written text or translate content between languages. Requirements depend on language pair, accuracy standards, and the client.

Data Entry and Online Research

Organize information, verify records, research public information, or update spreadsheets and databases.

Customer Support and Virtual Assistance

Support customers, manage inboxes, schedule appointments, maintain records, or assist teams remotely.

Freelance Digital Services

Offer writing, design, editing, web support, or other skills to clients online. Build a clear profile and portfolio.

Safety before applying

Protect yourself: Never pay an upfront fee to secure a job or release supposed earnings. Check the official employer website and confirm country eligibility and payment options. Prime Global does not guarantee selection, task availability, or income. If a listing looks suspicious, do not share passwords, one-time codes, or sensitive identity documents.
