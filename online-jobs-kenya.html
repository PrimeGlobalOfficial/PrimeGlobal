Online work categories to consider

AI Evaluation and Training

Projects may involve reviewing AI-generated answers, checking relevance, rating search results, or writing feedback. Some projects require strong English, subject expertise, and qualification tests.

Data Annotation

Label or categorize text, images, audio, or other data using project instructions. Accuracy, attention to detail, and passing an assessment are often important.

Transcription and Translation

Transcription converts recorded speech into text. Translation work requires suitable language skills and may include editing or quality checks. Confirm the languages requested for each project.

Customer Support

Remote support roles may include answering customer questions by email, chat, or phone. Check shift times, internet requirements, equipment, and whether the employer hires in Kenya.

Data Entry and Online Research

Tasks can include organizing records, checking information, spreadsheet updates, and research using public sources. Review the scope and payment terms before accepting work.

Freelance Digital Services

Offer writing, design, editing, virtual assistance, or technical services to clients. A clear profile, honest skills summary, and samples of work can help demonstrate your experience.

What to check before applying from Kenya

  • Country eligibility: confirm that the platform or specific project accepts applicants who live in Kenya.
  • Equipment: check whether a smartphone is sufficient or whether a laptop, headset, or quiet workspace is required.
  • Language and skills: review tests, experience requirements, and expected quality standards.
  • Payment: verify supported payment methods in Kenya, minimum withdrawal thresholds, fees, exchange rates, and payment schedules.
  • Task availability: registration does not guarantee approval, assignments, or a stable income.

How to avoid online job scams

  • Do not pay an upfront fee to obtain a job, unlock tasks, or release supposed earnings.
  • Use the official website and check the employer's identity before sharing personal documents.
  • Be cautious of guaranteed high income for little or no work.
  • Read the contract, privacy policy, and payment terms before agreeing to participate.
