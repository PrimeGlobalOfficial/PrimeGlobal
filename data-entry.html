Data Entry

Learn what online data entry work involves, common tasks, requirements, application processes, equipment needs and payment considerations.

What is Data Entry?

Data entry involves entering, updating, organizing or transferring information into digital systems, spreadsheets, databases or other software.

Online data-entry work can range from simple information entry to more detailed administrative and data-processing tasks.

Common Data Entry Tasks

  • Entering information into spreadsheets.
  • Updating databases and records.
  • Copying information between systems.
  • Checking records for missing information.
  • Formatting and organizing documents.
  • Product or inventory data entry.
  • Online research and information collection.
  • Document conversion and data processing.
  • Basic administrative support.

Who Can Apply?

Typical requirements may include:

  • Good typing accuracy.
  • Attention to detail.
  • Basic computer skills.
  • Ability to use spreadsheets.
  • Good written communication.
  • Ability to follow instructions.
  • Reliable internet connection.
  • Ability to meet deadlines.
Some entry-level tasks may not require previous professional experience, while more advanced positions can require spreadsheet, administrative or industry-specific experience.

Hardware & Software

  • Computer or laptop for most professional data-entry jobs.
  • Reliable internet connection.
  • Modern web browser.
  • Email account.
  • Spreadsheet software such as Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Word-processing software for document-based work.
Phone-only applicants: Basic data-entry tasks may sometimes be possible on a smartphone, but most serious remote data-entry jobs are easier and more practical with a computer or laptop. Always check the employer's requirements.

Skills That Can Help

  • Fast and accurate typing.
  • Microsoft Excel.
  • Google Sheets.
  • Data organization.
  • Online research.
  • Document formatting.
  • Basic spreadsheet formulas.
  • Attention to detail.
  • Time management.

Payments

Payment depends on the employer, project, contract, country and payment arrangement.

Possible payment methods can include:

  • PayPal
  • Payoneer
  • Airtm
  • Bank transfer
  • Other payment providers specified by the employer

Confirm the payment method, currency, payment schedule and withdrawal requirements before accepting work.

Country Availability

Remote data-entry positions can have geographic restrictions. Some employers accept applicants worldwide, while others recruit only from specific countries.

For Kenya: Check the job description carefully for Kenya eligibility before applying.

How to Avoid Data-Entry Scams

  • Do not pay a registration fee to obtain a job.
  • Be suspicious of guaranteed high earnings for very little work.
  • Verify the company's official website.
  • Do not send passwords or security codes to recruiters.
  • Check the company's contact details independently.
  • Read the contract or project terms before starting.
